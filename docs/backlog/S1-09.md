## Goal
Manage target companies and discover which ATS boards they use.

## Files
- `apps/api/app/integrations/ats/detect.py`
- `apps/api/app/api/companies.py`
- `apps/api/app/cli.py (companies commands)`
- `data/companies.seed.csv (header + 3 example rows)`
- `apps/api/tests/integrations/test_detect.py`
- `apps/api/tests/api/test_companies.py`

## Spec
`data/companies.seed.csv` columns: `name,website,priority,greenhouse,lever,ashby` (token columns may be blank). `jt companies import <csv>`: upsert companies and sources. `jt companies detect "<Company Name>"`: generate candidate slugs (lowercase, no spaces; hyphenated; with "inc/hq/ai/labs" removed) and call `exists()` on each ATS with a concurrency of 5. The first hit creates the source, and multiple hits are all kept. API: `GET/POST/PATCH /companies`, `POST /companies/{id}/detect`.

## Acceptance criteria
- detection on fixtures correctly finds a known slug and rejects a 404.
- Definition of Done in AGENTS.md §4.

## Test plan
respx: one ATS returns 200 for a candidate slug, the others 404; CSV import idempotency.

## Out of scope
Crawling external company lists (manual seed only for now).
